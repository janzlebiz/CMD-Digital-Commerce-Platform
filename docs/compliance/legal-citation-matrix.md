## 1. Statutory Architecture & Distinction of Authority

This matrix maps each statutory provision of the **Internet Transactions Act of 2023 (RA 11967)** applicable to the HCI CMD Digital Commerce Platform operating as an **e-retailer and online merchant**.

**Critical Analytical Distinction:**
- **Statutory Requirement:** A direct legal mandate enacted by Congress in RA 11967 or promulgated in its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). Non-compliance carries administrative fines, takedown orders, or blacklisting.
- **Recommended Platform Policy:** An internal operational target, SLA, or business design choice adopted by the platform to manage customer experience, logistics, or risk. An internal policy **MUST NEVER** be cited as a statutory law.

| **Section 8** | **Online Business Database (OBD)**  <br>The E-Commerce Bureau shall establish an Online Business Database containing contact details of digital platforms, e-marketplaces, e-retailers, and online merchants. | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT** | **Directly Applicable.** The business entity must register with the DTI E-Commerce Bureau OBD prior to or upon commercial launch. | System admin dashboard must store and display the DTI OBD Registration identifier once assigned. | **Statutory Mandate.** Compliance is required under DTI administrative rules. |

| **Section 23(a)** | **Price Disclosure & Price Tag Law**  <br>E-retailers and online merchants shall indicate prices of goods and services consistent with Article 81 of Republic Act No. 7394 (Consumer Act of the Philippines). | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT** | **Directly Applicable.** All prices for HCI CMD products, bundles, delivery, and services must be displayed in Philippine Peso (PHP / ₱). | Storefront and cart UI must calculate and display full, itemized prices before order finalization. Information must be in English and/or Filipino. | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT** | **Directly Applicable.** Products dispatched from Daet or branches must match product descriptions, lot specifications, and shelf-life horizons. | Catalog copy must accurately describe product volume (e.g. 65 mL), dilution directions, and packaging. Packing slips must verify item accuracy. | **Statutory Mandate.** Discrepant or substandard delivery constitutes a statutory violation. |
| **Section 23(c)** | **Digital Goods Standards**  <br>Ensure digital goods possess standard qualities of functionality, compatibility, interoperability, accessibility, continuity, and security. | **Conditionally Applicable** | Applies to any digital educational guides or downloadable consultation summaries provided to customers. | Digital health guides or event passes must be downloadable in standard accessible formats (e.g. PDF). | **Statutory Mandate.** Governs digital content delivery. |
| **Section 23(d)** | **Internal Redress Mechanism**  <br>E-retailers and online merchants shall maintain an accessible and efficient internal redress mechanism to handle consumer complaints. | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT** | **Directly Applicable.** The platform must provide a structured mechanism for customers to file complaints regarding defective items, non-delivery, or billing errors. | Implement a customer support ticketing portal with ticket tracking IDs, resolution logs, and timestamped communications. | **Statutory Mandate.** Maintaining a complaint channel is legally required. *Note: Internal response SLAs (e.g. 24h/48h) are platform policies.* |
| **Section 23(e)** | **Consumer Data Privacy**  <br>Take necessary precautions to protect consumer data privacy in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and comply with minimum security standards. | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT** | **Directly Applicable.** Cross-references RA 10173 as a statutory duty under e-commerce law. | Implement RBAC, HTTPS/TLS, session protection, and data minimization as required by privacy regulations. | **Statutory Mandate.** Direct legislative link between e-commerce operations and DPA compliance. |
| **Section 23(f)** | **Consumer Identification for Delivery**  <br>E-retailers and online merchants may require consumers to provide a valid email address or mobile phone number before completing transactions. | **STATUTORY RIGHT / PERMISSION** | **Directly Applicable.** Enables platform to require valid mobile phone numbers for order dispatch and branch pickup verification. | Checkout flow requires mobile phone number and recipient name. Enables SMS pickup code delivery and courier communication. | **Statutory Authorization.** Empowers merchant to mandate contact info to protect against fraudulent orders. |
| **Section 23(g)** | **Issuance of Invoices and Receipts**  <br>E-retailers and online merchants shall issue paper or electronic invoices or receipts for all sales. | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT** | **Directly Applicable.** Every online or branch pickup order must generate a compliant sales invoice. | Implement automated electronic sales invoice generation adhering to BIR and EOPT Act standards (serialized invoice number, merchant TIN, VAT details). | **Statutory Mandate.** Paper or digital invoices are legally required for all sales. |
| **Section 23 (IRR)**| **Merchant Identification & Physical Location**  <br>Online merchants must publish business information on their homepage, including registered corporate/trade name, geographic physical address, and contact details. | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT** | **Directly Applicable.** Platform footer and "About" surfaces must prominently state the business entity name, Daet main address, and contact phone/email. | Web layout must include permanent merchant disclosure banner in footer with DTI/BIR registration data and physical address. | **Statutory Mandate.** Prohibits anonymous or untraceable online sellers. |
| **Section 24** | **Internal Redress Mechanism & Exhaustion**  <br>An aggrieved party shall first utilize the internal redress mechanism before filing a complaint in court or with a government agency. The internal redress mechanism is deemed exhausted if unresolved after seven (7) calendar days from filing. | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT** | **Directly Applicable.** Establishes a 7-calendar-day statutory exhaustion window before consumers may escalate complaints to the DTI or regular courts. | Complaint tickets must track elapsed time from submission. If a complaint is not resolved within 7 days, the ticket must notify the manager of impending regulatory escalation. | **Statutory Procedural Rule.** **CRITICAL:** This 7-day period is an exhaustion rule for dispute escalation, NOT an unconditional right to refund or return opened dietary supplements. |
| **Section 29** | **Administrative Penalties & Takedown Orders**  <br>DTI Secretary is empowered to issue compliance orders, takedown orders, and impose administrative fines (₱5,000 to ₱1,000,000) for violations. | **STATUTORY PENALTY** | **Applicable to Non-Compliance.** Non-compliant platforms face takedown orders or blacklisting. | Architecture and content publishing policies must maintain strict compliance to eliminate risk of administrative sanctions. | **Statutory Sanction.** Legal risk enforcement provision. |

## 3. Statutory vs. Platform Policy Clarification Table

To prevent confusion between statutory mandates and internal operational policies, the following distinctions are established:

| Operational Item | Platform Policy Designation | Statutory Reference | Legal Distinction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Support SLA: First Response in 24–48 Hours** | **RECOMMENDED PLATFORM POLICY** | *None in statute.* | RA 11967 requires an "efficient and accessible" redress mechanism, but does not prescribe a 24-hour SLA. The SLA is an internal operational target. |
| **7-Day Redress Exhaustion** | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT** | RA 11967 Section 24 | The law establishes that after 7 calendar days of an unresolved internal complaint, the consumer is legally entitled to file an action with DTI or court. It is **NOT** a 7-day unconditional return policy for opened bottles. |
| **Product Return Policy (Opened Supplements)** | **RECOMMENDED PLATFORM POLICY (Governed by Consumer Act)** | RA 7394 (Consumer Act) | Under general consumer protection, return/replacement of sealed food supplements is limited to damaged seals, defects, or mismatches. Hygiene restrictions apply to unsealed consumable liquids. |
| **Branch Pickup Holding Window (5 Days)** | **RECOMMENDED PLATFORM POLICY** | *None in statute.* | An operational choice to manage branch inventory turnover. Not mandated by law. |
| **Electronic Invoice Format** | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT** | RA 11967 Section 23(g) & RA 11976 (EOPT) | The issuance of a sales invoice is an express statutory requirement under both e-commerce and tax statutes. |
