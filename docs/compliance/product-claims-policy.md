# Product Claims, Marketing & Editorial Compliance Policy

**Policy Reference:** COMP-POL-002 (Remediated)  
**Platform:** HCI CMD Digital Commerce Platform  
**Effective Date:** Phase 0 Baseline (2026-09-25)  
**Target Entities:** Marketing Staff, Content Editors, Naturopathic Practitioners, Customer Service Agents, Branch Managers  
**Regulatory Baseline:** Republic Act No. 9711, RA 7394, RA 11967, DOH AO 2014-0030, FDA Circular No. 2015-003  

---

## 1. Purpose, Legal Authority & Scope

This policy enforces strict compliance with Philippine consumer protection laws (RA 7394), the Food and Drug Administration Act of 2009 (RA 9711), the Internet Transactions Act of 2023 (RA 11967), and DOH/FDA advertising circulars.

This policy applies unconditionally across:
1. Product titles, descriptions, and packaging photos on the storefront.
2. Educational blog posts, wellness guides, and social media links.
3. Event and symposium descriptions, speaker agendas, and promotional banners.
4. Wellness and naturopathic consultation booking forms, intake summaries, and practitioner communications.
5. Automated SMS, email, and push notifications sent to customers.
6. Customer service live chat, support tickets, and branch counter communications.

## 3. Mandatory Statutory Disclaimers

### 3.1 Filipino Statutory Warning (Primary)
The following statement must be permanently embedded, prominently visible, and non-removable on **every** digital surface that references HCI CMD or related dietary supplements (FDA Circular No. 2015-003):

> **"MAHALAGANG PAALALA: ANG HCI CELL MINERAL DROPS AY HINDI GAMOT AT HINDI DAPAT GAMITING PANGGAMOT SA ANUMANG URI NG SAKIT."**

- **Typography Rules:** High contrast (e.g., dark text on warning-tinted or neutral white background), bold typeface, minimum font size matching body text (never fine print or obscure footers).
- **Placement:** Must appear immediately adjacent to the product title and "Add to Cart" / Purchase action, and at the head or foot of every educational article discussing mineral benefits.

### 3.2 English Statutory Disclaimer (Secondary)
> **"NO APPROVED THERAPEUTIC CLAIMS"**

Must appear directly beneath the product title and on all digital transaction sales invoices.

### 3.3 Vulnerable Demographics Advisory
> **"Precautions: For adult dietary supplementation. Pregnant or lactating women, individuals with chronic kidney disease, severe electrolyte restrictions, or persons undergoing medical treatment should consult their licensed physician before use. Keep out of reach of children."**

## 4. Prohibited Claims Matrix (Blacklist)

Any content containing the following statements, keywords, or implications is **strictly prohibited** from publication. The digital platform's content publishing pipeline will enforce automated keyword filters and editorial sign-off to reject these claims:

| Prohibited Category | Explicit Forbidden Claims / Phrases | Regulatory & Policy Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Disease Cures & Reversals** | "Cures diabetes", "lowers high blood sugar permanently", "eliminates hypertension", "cures kidney stones", "dissolves cysts", "anti-cancer mineral", "cures arthritis". | Violates RA 9711 & RA 7394. Food supplements cannot claim therapeutic or curative effects. |
| **Ophthalmic / Eye Usage** | "CMD eye drops", "clear cataracts naturally", "cure glaucoma", "natural eye wash", "apply 1 drop directly into the eyes". | **Severe Safety Hazard & Platform Compliance Policy:** Hypertonic mineral solutions placed in human eyes risk severe chemical irritation and corneal injury. While FDA Advisory 2019-363 and 2020-1389 adjudicated other product brands, the platform adopts a conservative non-negotiable policy prohibiting all ophthalmic use. |
| **Medical Replacement** | "Stop taking your prescription maintenance medication", "natural substitute for insulin", "replace heart medicine with mineral drops". | Reckless endangerment of human life and unlawful practice of medicine under RA 2382. |
| **Absolutist Health Promises** | "Guaranteed 100% cure", "miracle drop", "instant healing", "zero side effects guarantee". | Deceptive and misleading advertising under RA 11967 and RA 7394. |
| **Mucosal / Invasive Administration** | "Nasal drops for sinus cure", "ear drops for otitis media". | Unapproved routes of administration for a food supplement. |

## 5. Permitted Claims Matrix (Whitelist)

The platform may only publish statements grounded in verified nutritional biochemistry and FDA food supplement guidelines:

| Permitted Category | Compliant Formulation / Statement Example | Guidance Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Solar Harvest Heritage** | "Concentrated from the mineral-rich waters of the Great Salt Lake, Utah, through natural solar evaporation." | Truthful manufacturing origin statement. |

## 6. Directions for Use & Storage Guidelines

1. **Directions for Use:**
   - Dilute in liquids. Do not consume large volumes undiluted due to concentrated mineral salinity.
   - Standard Adult Serving: Add 5 to 10 drops into a glass of drinking water, fruit juice, or beverage, 2 to 3 times daily.
   - For remineralizing drinking water: Add 20 to 30 drops per gallon (approx. 3.8 liters) of drinking water.
2. **Storage Information:**
   - Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight at temperatures not exceeding 30°C.
   - Highly concentrated mineral brine is naturally self-preserving and does not require refrigeration.
   - Minor mineral crystallization around the dropper tip is normal for high-salinity natural ionic solutions and does not indicate product spoilage.

## 7. Naturopathic Consultation & Event Boundary Rules

1. **Consultation Boundary:**
   - Naturopathic and wellness consultations conducted through the platform are **wellness and lifestyle guidance sessions**, not formal clinical medical diagnoses.
   - Practitioners must not issue medical prescriptions, alter prescription pharmaceuticals prescribed by registered physicians, or promise disease eradication using HCI CMD.
   - The booking flow must feature an explicit informed consent banner:
     > *"Our wellness consultations provide holistic lifestyle, dietary, and nutritional education. They do not constitute licensed medical diagnosis or clinical treatment. Always maintain routine care with your primary physician."*
2. **Symposium & Seminar Rules:**
   - Speakers, guest lecturers, and video recordings presented at platform events must abide by the claims policy.
   - Testimonial videos or articles claiming personal recovery from terminal diseases must not be published on the digital storefront or mobile PWA.
