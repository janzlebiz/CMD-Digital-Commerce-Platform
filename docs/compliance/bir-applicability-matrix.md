## 1. Statutory Architecture & EOPT Framework

Under the **Ease of Paying Taxes (EOPT) Act (Republic Act No. 11976)**, signed into law on January 5, 2024, the Philippine tax compliance framework for sales of goods and services underwent fundamental modernization:
1. **The "Invoice" as Sole Primary Substantiation:** The EOPT Act eliminated the historical statutory distinction between "Sales Invoices" (for goods) and "Official Receipts" (for services). An **Invoice** (whether electronic or physical) is now the sole primary document supporting VAT deduction and commercial transactions across both goods sales (HCI CMD bottles) and services (wellness consultations).
2. **Standardization of Mandatory Invoice Details:** Under Section 237 of the NIRC as amended by the EOPT Act, all invoices must state:
   - Serialized Invoice Number.
   - Date of Transaction.
   - Merchant Registered Name, Registered Address, and TIN.
   - Buyer's Name, Address, and TIN (for sales to registered businesses or sales above statutory thresholds).
   - Description of Goods or Services, Quantity, Unit Cost, and Total Amount.
   - Breakdown of VATable Sales, VAT Amount (12%), VAT-Exempt Sales, and Zero-Rated Sales (or declaration of Non-VAT Percentage Tax if applicable).
3. **Amended Retention Period (NIRC Section 235):** RA 11976 amended Section 235 of the NIRC to provide a **five (5) year** preservation period for books of accounts and other accounting records, replacing the previous 10-year regime.

## 3. Data Domain Retention Classification Matrix

To enforce rigorous evidentiary standards and eliminate unsupported retention generalizations, the platform establishes domain-specific retention policies separating statutory mandates from recommended internal policies:

| Data Domain | Examples | Applicable Legal Standard / Authority | Retention Period | Rationale & Legal Classification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Statutory Tax & Accounting Records** | Sales Invoices, Credit Notes, BIR Form 2307, Settlement Ledgers, Tax Books | NIRC Section 235, as amended by RA 11976 (EOPT Act) | **Five (5) Years**, reckoned according to amended Section 235 | **STATUTORY REQUIREMENT.** Preservation of books of accounts and accounting records as mandated by amended NIRC Section 235. |
| **Operational E-Commerce Records** | Order packing slips, courier dispatch notes, cart line items, pickup logs | Commercial Best Practice | **Three (3) Years** | **RECOMMENDATION / INTERNAL POLICY.** Internal operational policy for resolving commercial disputes and logistics claims. Not an express statutory requirement. |
| **Wellness & Consultation Records** | Health intake questionnaires, practitioner clinical notes, hydration logs | RA 10173 (DPA) Proportionality & Clinical Continuity | **Five (5) Years** from last consultation | **RECOMMENDATION / INTERNAL POLICY.** Internal clinical continuity policy balancing client wellness history against DPA data minimization. Not an express statutory requirement. |
| **Security & Authentication Logs** | User login timestamps, failed auth attempts, IP access logs, API audit events | NPC Circular 16-04 (Security Guidelines) | **Twelve (12) Months** | **RECOMMENDATION / INTERNAL POLICY.** Recommended technical security control for forensic investigation while limiting data liability. Not an express statutory requirement. |
| **Marketing Consent Records** | Newsletter opt-ins, SMS promotional checkboxes, unsubscribe timestamps | RA 10173 Section 11 (General Principles) | **Active Consent Duration** | **LEGAL COMPLIANCE BASELINE.** Retained until consent is revoked by the data subject, after which data is purged from promotional dispatch lists. |
| **Abandoned Shopping Carts** | Incomplete checkout sessions, temporary reservation holds | Data Minimization Principle | **30 Calendar Days** | **RECOMMENDATION / INTERNAL POLICY.** Purged automatically to prevent retention of unnecessary consumer telemetry. |
