## 2. Distinction of Regulatory & Authorization Instruments

It is critical not to conflate separate legal and regulatory authorizations. In Philippine administrative law, the following instruments serve distinct legal functions:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        REGULATORY & COMMERCIAL AUTHORIZATION LAYERS                    │
├───────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. FDA Product Registration   │ FR-4000008713595. Certifies that the specific food     │
│    (Food CPR / FR)            │ supplement formulation is registered for human intake. │
├───────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Establishment License to   │ LTO (License to Operate). Held by the corporate entity │
│    Operate (LTO)              │ (Health Code International Corporation) as food        │
│                               │ distributor / wholesaler / importer.                   │
├───────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Distributor Authorization  │ Commercial contract / dealership agreement between     │
│    Agreement                  │ Health Code International Corp. and the independent    │
│                               │ Camarines Norte business entity.                       │
├───────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Manufacturer Authorization │ Supply agreement between US manufacturer / harvest     │
│    / Harvest Source           │ source (e.g. Great Salt Lake extraction) and HCI.      │
├───────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. Local Business Entity      │ DTI/SEC registration, Mayor's Business Permits for the │
│    Registrations              │ six branches, and BIR Form 2303 Tax Registration.      │
└───────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. FDA SKU Evidence Matrix

In commercial distribution across Camarines Norte, the business intends to offer various package presentations (e.g., 65 mL dropper bottles, 30 mL dropper bottles, and promotional bundles).

Under FDA Center for Food Regulation and Research (CFRR) procedures, an FR (Certificate of Product Registration for Food) is issued to a specific product formulation and brand. Packaging sizes (e.g., 30 mL, 65 mL, 120 mL) are typically specified within the packaging declaration or annex of the CPR, or in some instances, under separate variant notifications.

## 4. Product Identity, Composition & Packaging Details

### 4.1 Product Identity
- **Full Registered Name:** HCI CMD / Cell Mineral Drops (Ionic Mineral Concentrate) Food Supplement Drops
- **Brand:** HCI CMD™
- **Registrant:** Health Code International Corporation
- **Statutory Status:** Processed Food Product — Food Supplement
- **Approved Claims:** None. The FDA registration explicitly carries: **"NO APPROVED THERAPEUTIC CLAIMS"**.

### 4.2 Ingredients & Composition (Verified vs. Manufacturer Claims)
- **Verified Compositional Nature (FDA/Nutrition Facts):** Concentrated aqueous mineral solution containing dietary magnesium, chloride, potassium, and sulfate, derived from natural saline lake brine.
- **Manufacturer Technical Claims (Unverified by independent lab assay):** Contains 72+ naturally occurring ionic trace minerals; ~99.5% sodium removed via solar evaporation.
- **Regulatory Truth:** While marketed as containing trace minerals, it is legally approved solely as an oral dietary supplement to supplement normal diet, not to alter human physiology or cure metabolic ailments.

### 4.3 Directions for Use & Storage
- **Directions (Standard Distributor/Reseller Guidance):** Dilute 5 to 10 drops in a glass of potable water or beverage, 2 to 3 times daily. Do not consume large volumes undiluted due to extreme mineral salinity.
- **Storage:** Store at temperatures not exceeding 30°C in a dry place protected from direct sunlight. Keep bottle tightly closed.

## 5. Non-Negotiable Gate Requirements for Phase 1 Onboarding

Before any individual SKU is enabled for commercial purchase in Phase 2:
1. **Physical CPR Inspection:** The business owner must upload a complete PDF scan of the physical Certificate of Product Registration for FR-4000008713595, including the manufacturer details, product specifications, and approved packaging presentations.
2. **Batch / Lot Traceability:** Every commercial shipment accepted at the Daet central warehouse must display the active FR registration number, lot number, manufacturing date, and expiry date (within the March 18, 2028 validity horizon).
3. **Packaging Art Verification:** Physical bottle labels must be inspected to verify that the approved Filipino disclaimer (`"MAHALAGANG PAALALA: ANG HCI CELL MINERAL DROPS AY HINDI GAMOT..."`) is pre-printed on the label.
