## 2. Distinction of Regulatory & Authorization Instruments

It is critical not to conflate separate legal and regulatory authorizations. In Philippine administrative law, the following instruments serve distinct legal functions:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        REGULATORY & COMMERCIAL AUTHORIZATION LAYERS                    │
├───────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. FDA Product Registration   │ FR-4000008713595. Certifies that the specific food     │
│    Record                     │ supplement formulation is registered for human intake. │
├───────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Packaging / Presentation   │ Official registration annex or packaging specification │
│    Evidence                   │ establishing approved bottle volumes (e.g. 65 mL).     │
├───────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Establishment License to   │ LTO (License to Operate). Held by corporate entity     │
│    Operate (LTO)              │ (Health Code International Corporation) as food        │
│                               │ distributor / wholesaler / importer.                   │
├───────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Distributor Authorization  │ Commercial contract / dealership agreement between     │
│    Agreement                  │ Health Code International Corp. and the independent    │
│                               │ Camarines Norte business entity.                       │
├───────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. Local Business Entity      │ DTI/SEC registration, Mayor's Business Permits for the │
│    Registrations              │ six branches, and BIR Form 2303 Tax Registration.      │
└───────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

*Terminology Note:* In Philippine food regulation, food registrations are officially designated by the FDA as an **FR-** number (e.g., `FR-4000008713595`). While commonly referred to in the trade as a "Certificate of Product Registration (CPR)", the precise primary statutory term is **FDA Product Registration**. This documentation uses precise terminology to distinguish product registration from establishment LTOs, packaging annexes, and dealership authorizations.

## 3. FDA SKU Evidence Matrix

In commercial distribution across Camarines Norte, the business intends to offer various package presentations (e.g. 65 mL dropper bottles, 30 mL dropper bottles, and promotional bundles).

**Critical Regulatory Boundary:**  
The official FDA verification record confirms the product formulation and brand under `FR-4000008713595` and records the packaging description as **"WHITE OPAQUE PLASTIC BOTTLE"**. However, the public verification summary does **not** specify individual milliliter volume allocations (such as 65 mL or 30 mL). 

Under FDA Center for Food Regulation and Research (CFRR) procedures, packaging sizes are approved within the registration documentation/annex. Therefore, **we CANNOT infer packaging authorization from the existence of the FDA product-level registration alone**. 

The commercial presentations must NOT be represented as verified or FDA-cleared until the physical registration annex is inspected.

## 4. Product Identity, Composition & Packaging Details

### 4.1 Product Identity
- **Full Registered Name:** CELL MINERAL DROPS (IONIC MINERAL CONCENTRATE) FOOD SUPPLEMENT DROPS
- **Brand:** HCI CMD™
- **Registrant:** HEALTH CODE INTERNATIONAL CORPORATION
- **Statutory Status:** Processed Food Product — Food Supplement (Medium Risk Food Product)
- **Approved Claims:** None. The FDA registration explicitly carries: **"Registered as Food Supplement with NO APPROVED THERAPEUTIC CLAIMS"**.
- **Recorded Packaging Description:** WHITE OPAQUE PLASTIC BOTTLE

### 4.2 Ingredients & Composition (Verified vs. Manufacturer Claims)
- **Verified Compositional Nature (FDA Registration):** Ionic mineral concentrate food supplement drops.
- **Manufacturer Technical Claims (Unverified by independent lab assay in this repository):** Concentrated aqueous mineral solution containing dietary magnesium, chloride, potassium, and sulfate, derived from natural Great Salt Lake saline brine; low sodium.
- **Regulatory Truth:** Approved solely as an oral dietary supplement to supplement normal diet, not to alter human physiology or cure diseases.

### 4.3 Directions for Use & Storage
- **Directions (Distributor Guidance):** Dilute 5 to 10 drops in a glass of potable water or beverage, 2 to 3 times daily. Do not consume large volumes undiluted due to extreme mineral salinity.
- **Storage:** Store at temperatures not exceeding 30°C in a dry place protected from direct sunlight. Keep bottle tightly closed.
