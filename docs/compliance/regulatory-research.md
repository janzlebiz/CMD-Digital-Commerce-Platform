## 1. Executive Summary

This document establishes the verified regulatory framework governing the commercialization, advertising, health consultation, and distribution of **HCI CMD (Cell Mineral Drops)** in the Philippines, specifically for an independent distribution business operating across six physical branches in Camarines Norte (Daet, Labo, Paracale, Panganiban, Capalonga, Sta. Elena).

Under Philippine statutory laws and regulatory agency directives:
1. **Product Categorization:** HCI CMD is legally classified and regulated as a **Food / Dietary Supplement (Processed Food Product)** under Republic Act No. 9711 (Food and Drug Administration Act of 2009) and DOH Administrative Order No. 2014-0030. It is **NOT** a pharmaceutical drug, medicine, or medical device.
2. **Mandatory Disclaimers:** All marketing collateral, website copy, product detail pages, and advertisements must conspicuously display the mandatory Philippine FDA disclaimer in Filipino (*FDA Circular No. 2015-003* / *ASC Circular 2018-016*):
   > **"MAHALAGANG PAALALA: ANG (PANGALAN NG PRODUKTO / HCI CMD) AY HINDI GAMOT AT HINDI DAPAT GAMITING PANGGAMOT SA ANUMANG URI NG SAKIT."**
   Along with the standard English designation:
   > **"NO APPROVED THERAPEUTIC CLAIMS"**
3. **Strict Prohibition on Medical Claims:** Neither the platform, practitioners, nor distribution personnel may claim, suggest, or imply that HCI CMD diagnoses, treats, cures, mitigates, or prevents any human illness, disease, infection, metabolic disorder, or physiological abnormality.
4. **FDA Safety Warnings on Administration:** The FDA Philippines has issued explicit public health advisories (including *FDA Advisory No. 2019-363*, *2019-189*, *2020-1389*) warning against marketing mineral drop concentrates as ophthalmic (eye) drops or ear solutions. Using hypertonic mineral drop solutions in the eyes can cause severe corneal irritation, chemical burn, ulceration, or permanent blindness. All platform content must prohibit unapproved routes of administration.

## 2. Regulatory Matrix: Five-Tier Claim Classification

Every statement published or transacted on the platform must be categorized into one of five rigorous tiers:

| Tier | Category | Definition | Platform Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier A** | **Verified Regulatory Facts** | Facts verified through primary government agency issuances, official gazettes, and official CPR (Certificate of Product Registration) records. | Permitted. Must be referenced accurately without extrapolation. |
| **Tier B** | **Manufacturer Technical Claims** | Technical specifications provided by the mineral harvest source (e.g., Mineral Resources International / Great Salt Lake origin, solar evaporation process, mineral composition analysis). | Permitted only as nutritional/ingredient facts. Cannot assert health disease treatment. |
| **Tier C** | **Distributor / Reseller Claims** | Commercial statements made by Health Code International (HCI) or distributors regarding business opportunity, packaging, or recommended beverage dilution. | Permitted if non-therapeutic and compliant with consumer truth-in-advertising laws. |
| **Tier D** | **Educational Statements** | General nutritional and physiological science (e.g., role of magnesium in electrolyte balance and normal muscle function as established by FNRI/WHO/Codex Alimentarius). | Permitted provided they do not link HCI CMD directly to curing diseases. |
| **Tier E** | **Unverified / Non-Approved Claims** | Any assertion that HCI CMD cures hypertension, diabetes, cancer, kidney stones, cataracts, glaucoma, or acts as an eye drop / topical cure. | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**. Instant content rejection. |

## 3. Product Identity & Technical Composition

### 3.1 Commercial & Brand Identity
- **Product Name:** HCI CMD / Cell Mineral Drops
- **Brand / Distributor Organization:** Health Code International (HCI)
- **Primary Source / Harvest Location:** Northern Arm of the Great Salt Lake, Utah, United States of America.
- **Extraction & Manufacturing Method:** Multi-stage solar concentration and precipitation process. Natural solar evaporation removes approximately 99.5% of the sodium chloride (halite crystallization), leaving a dense, hyper-concentrated magnesium and trace mineral ionic liquid.
- **Physical Characteristics:** Clear to pale straw-colored, highly concentrated, odourless, dense aqueous mineral solution with a characteristically strong, bitter, mineral/metallic taste if tasted undiluted.

### 3.2 Product Variants Documented in Philippine Trade
1. **HCI CMD 65 mL (Standard Bottle):** High-density dropper bottle for daily household and dilution use.
2. **HCI CMD 30 mL (Travel / Intro Size):** Portable dropper bottle.
3. **HCI CMD 15 mL / Sample Packs (Reported in Reseller Channels):** Mini dropper format.
*Audit Note:* All authorized SKUs for the platform must match the business owner’s physical warehouse and distributor packing list verified against FDA Certificates of Product Registration (CPR).

### 3.3 Elemental & Mineral Profile (Nutritional / Technical Formulation)
Based on Great Salt Lake brine mineral extraction analyses:
- **Magnesium ($Mg^{2+}$):** Primary dominant cation (~100 mg per 1 mL / ~20–30 drops).
- **Chloride ($Cl^-$):** Primary dominant anion (~300–350 mg per 1 mL).
- **Potassium ($K^+$):** Electrolyte present in natural ionic equilibrium.
- **Sulfate ($SO_4^{2-}$):** Natural mineral sulfate present in lake concentrate.
- **Sodium ($Na^+$):** Reduced naturally by ~99.5% during crystallization so it remains a low-sodium supplement.
- **Ionic Trace Minerals (72+ trace elements):** Naturally occurring trace deposits including boron, zinc, selenium, lithium, manganese, chromium, and others in trace/microgram parts-per-billion quantities.

## 4. Primary Regulatory Issuances & Statutory Framework

### 4.1 Republic Act No. 9711 (Food and Drug Administration Act of 2009)
- **Statutory Authority:** The FDA Philippines has sole jurisdiction over the manufacture, importation, exportation, sale, offering for sale, distribution, transfer, promotion, advertising, or sponsorship of health products.
- **Prohibited Act (Section 11):** The manufacture, sale, offer for sale, or advertising of any health product that is adulterated, misbranded, or unregistered.
- **Classification:** Processed Food / Dietary Supplement.
- **Penal Sanctions:** Confiscation, administrative fines up to ₱5,000,000, revocation of business permits, and criminal liability.

### 4.2 DOH Administrative Order No. 2014-0030 & FDA Circular No. 2013-010
- **Labeling Regulations for Food Supplements:**
  - Requires conspicuous labeling of the phrase **"FOOD SUPPLEMENT"** with minimum height specifications.
  - Requires the mandatory advisory: **"NO APPROVED THERAPEUTIC CLAIMS"**.
  - Must state the recommended serving size, frequency of intake, and instructions for dilution.
  - Mandatory precautionary statement: **"Not recommended for children, pregnant, and lactating women"** (unless specifically certified by clinical safety evaluation for such demographics).

### 4.3 FDA Memorandum Circular No. 2015-003 & ASC Circular 2018-016
- **Tagalog Advisory Mandate:**
  - Requires all broadcast, print, outdoor, digital, e-commerce, and point-of-sale promotional materials to state:
    > *"MAHALAGANG PAALALA: ANG (PANGALAN NG PRODUKTO) AY HINDI GAMOT AT HINDI DAPAT GAMITING PANGGAMOT SA ANUMANG URI NG SAKIT."*
  - Digital Display Requirements: The text must be legible, uncropped, contrasting against the background, and prominently positioned adjacent to the product title and price.

### 4.4 FDA Public Health Advisories on Mineral Drops
- **FDA Advisory No. 2019-363 ("CMD Natural Eye Solution"):**
  - The FDA strictly warned the public against an unregistered drug marketed as "CMD Natural Eye Solution 15 mL".
  - *Finding:* Mineral drop concentrates diluted or repackaged into eye drop bottles are unapproved drug preparations that pose severe ophthalmic hazards.
- **FDA Advisory No. 2020-1389 ("Mineral Blend Food Supplement Drops"):**
  - Confirmed that while mineral drops may hold valid food supplement registration, promoting or recommending them for instillation into human eyes constitutes unlawful misbranding and poses risks of corneal erosion and blindness.
- **Platform Compliance Constraint:** The e-commerce store, product descriptions, FAQs, staff, and naturopathic consultations are strictly forbidden from recommending HCI CMD as an ophthalmic solution or nasal instillation.
